About RightAware

Why RightAware exists

Millions of Nigerians face police stops, unlawful evictions, workplace exploitation, and consumer extortion without knowing the protections and statutory duties the law already provides. RightAware translates Nigerian constitutional and statutory law into plain, accessible, usable civic power.

Institutional Foundation

Our Core Platform Pillars

RightAware operates at the intersection of open civic data, legal literacy, and public accountability.

Mission

Publish free, plain-language rights guides, connect citizens directly to verified legal aid and public ombudsmen, and engineer trustworthy civic-tech tools with verifiable statutory citations.

Vision

A Nigeria where every person — regardless of income, geography, or formal schooling — can understand the law, assert their constitutional protections, and seek lawful redress without fear.

Who We Are

An independent Nigerian civic-tech initiative combining legal awareness content, verified referral channels, and open-source civic tools — not a law firm, not a government agency.

Strategic Objectives

What we set out to accomplish

Six measurable commitments guiding our engineering, editorial standards, and community outreach.

Simplify the law

Translate complex constitutional provisions and statutory acts into clear, accessible English and Naijá Pidgin, expanding into major Nigerian languages over time.

Bridge to verified help

Maintain direct referral pathways to accredited legal practitioners, university law clinics, human rights desks, and statutory complaints channels.

Strengthen citizenship

Empower citizens to participate actively in democratic processes, demand institutional accountability, and navigate administrative procedures lawfully.

Protect factual accuracy

Enforce strict editorial verification across every guide and law summary. Clearly badge verification status — never fabricate statutes, citations, or legal outcomes.

Stay reachable offline

Optimize our core platform for low-bandwidth 2G/3G connections and budget smartphones, providing offline caching and lightweight printable checklists.

Grow in the open

Develop content frameworks, platform architecture, and community partnerships in the open with auditable version control and transparent documentation.

Institutional Boundaries

Civic Mandate vs. Statutory Non-Scope

Clarity regarding our platform capabilities and legal boundaries is essential for public trust.

RightAware Platform Scope

What we actively build, publish, and maintain
  • Free Public Awareness Library

    Curated legal rights guides with direct references to the 1999 Constitution, ACJA 2015, and the Police Act 2020.

  • Verified Referral Infrastructure

    A structured directory linking citizens to accredited pro-bono clinics, public defenders, and statutory complaint desks.

  • Mobile-First & Low-Bandwidth Tools

    Offline-capable search, downloadable evidentiary printables, and emergency hotline dispatch cards built for any device.

  • Transparent Source Integrity

    Every piece of content carries a transparent editorial badge (Verified, Draft, or Awaiting Review) to prevent misinformation.

Statutory Non-Scope

Strict legal boundaries and what we cannot do
  • Not a Law Firm

    RightAware does not practice law, solicit clients, or provide individualized legal representation in court.

  • Not Legal Advice

    Guides are for general civic education only. Specific legal disputes require formal consultation with a licensed legal practitioner.

  • Not an Emergency Dispatch Service

    In life-threatening situations, active violent crimes, or immediate physical emergencies, citizens must call 112 directly.

  • No Unverified Legal Claims

    We do not certify lawyer qualifications without secondary confirmation against the Supreme Court of Nigeria roll.

Statutory Integrity & Data Privacy

Institutional accountability protocols governing legal educational content and citizen information.

Legal Information Disclaimer

RightAware provides public legal information for civic education, not private legal counsel or representation. Nigerian laws, state tenancy regulations, and criminal procedure codes vary across jurisdictions and evolve over time.

Guides marked AWAITING VERIFICATION, DRAFT, or DEMO are shared for public review and must be verified against official gazettes or certified legal counsel before reliance in formal litigation. In immediate emergencies, contact official emergency dispatchers at 112.

NDPR Privacy & Data Sovereignty

RightAware adheres strictly to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and NDPR regulations. We collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver civic services: account authentication, submitted incident reports, and direct consultation requests you choose to initiate.

Submitted consultation requests and verification records are stored securely and never exposed publicly or monetized for advertising. Directory listings display only information explicitly submitted by practitioners for public civic outreach.

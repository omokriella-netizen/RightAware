About Us

Why RightAware exists

Millions of Nigerians face police stops, evictions, dismissals and scams without knowing the protections and duties the law already gives them. RightAware turns legal knowledge into plain, usable power.

🎯

Mission

Publish free, plain-language rights guides, connect people to real help, and build trustworthy civic-tech tools (library, directory, demo AI) with verified sources.

🔭

Vision

A Nigeria where every person — regardless of income, location or schooling — can understand the law well enough to act on it.

🧭

Who we are

A Nigerian civic-tech initiative combining legal-awareness content, referral pathways and open technology — not a law firm, not a government agency.

Objectives

What we set out to do

📚 Simplify the law

Explain rights, duties and procedures in English, Pidgin and major Nigerian languages over time.

🤝 Bridge to help

Refer users to verified lawyers, clinics, support services and complaint channels.

🗳 Strengthen citizenship

Promote voting, accountability and peaceful civic participation.

🔎 Protect accuracy

Mark every item verified, draft, or placeholder — never fabricate laws, people or numbers.

📴 Stay reachable

Keep core guides readable offline and usable on low-end phones and poor networks.

🌍 Grow openly

Build content, tools and partnerships in the open with clear documentation.

How it works

Learn → Act → Get help

✅ For citizens

  • Learn: search or browse 22 rights guides, laws, videos and FAQs.
  • Act: follow checklists — document, keep evidence, write formal complaints.
  • Get help: report concerns, browse professionals, contact support bodies.
  • Stay: save guides offline, subscribe for tips, ask the AI demo.

✅ Who it serves

  • Everyday citizens — workers, tenants, students, traders, drivers.
  • Vulnerable groups — women, children, persons with disabilities.
  • Communities facing environmental or land pressures.
  • Lawyers, clinics and NGOs reaching people who need them.

✅ RightAware IS

  • A free awareness library with source hints to verify
  • A referral bridge to lawyers, clinics and support bodies
  • A civic-tech platform: search, directory UI, AI placeholder
  • Built mobile-first for phone, tablet and desktop

⛔ RightAware IS NOT

  • A law firm — it does not represent anyone
  • Legal advice — content is general information only
  • A replacement for emergency services
  • A source of verified lawyer credentials (yet)
Trust & responsibility

Legal-information disclaimer

⚖️ RightAware provides general legal information, not legal advice and not representation. Laws differ by state and change over time. Content marked AWAITING VERIFICATION, DRAFT, DEMO or PLACEHOLDER must be confirmed against official sources or a qualified legal practitioner before reliance. In emergencies, contact official emergency services first.
Privacy

How your data is handled

RightAware stores only what the service needs to work: your account details, the applications administrators review, messages you send us, and consultation requests you choose to make. During review, applications are visible only to administrators — your contact details, documents and messages are never shown on public pages or to other users. A consultation request is shared with the professional you selected. Public directory listings show only the profile fields a professional fills in themselves. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

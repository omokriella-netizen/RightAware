Emergency / help contacts

Save these VERIFY LOCALLY

v1 does not hard-code phone numbers. Confirm current official numbers for your state before an emergency.

🚨 Immediate danger

Contact official emergency services or move somewhere safe first. Keep your phone charged and share your live location with someone trusted.

🏥 Medical + evidence

After assault: seek medical care promptly and keep the medical report — it matters for any complaint or case.

📝 Document early

Names, numbers, plates, times, photos, witnesses. Evidence collected in the first hours is the most valuable.

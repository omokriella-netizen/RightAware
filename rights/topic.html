Key points

    What to do if this affects you

    1. Write down what happened: dates, places, names, and what was said — while it is fresh.
    2. Keep evidence: receipts, messages, photos, letters, medical reports. Store copies safely.
    3. Raise it in writing with the other party or institution first, with a clear request and deadline.
    4. If unresolved or urgent, use Get Help to find reporting and referral paths.
